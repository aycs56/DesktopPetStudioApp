OPTIONAL CALENDAR SYNC

Let a familiar companion remember the important moments

You may connect Google Calendar and turn upcoming events into local desktop-pet reminders. Sign-in happens in your default browser, and the app reads only the calendar events needed to create reminders.

  • Choose a 30-, 60-, or 180-day sync window
  • Sync again when the app starts and every day at 00:00
  • Disconnect or change accounts anytime without deleting existing local reminders
