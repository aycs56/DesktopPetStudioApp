# Homepage launch date — user story

## US-HOME-LAUNCH-01: See the current planned release date

As a homepage visitor, I want the launch banner to show the updated planned
Steam release date before launch, so that I am not shown an offer countdown
before the offer has started.

### Acceptance intent

- The schedule begins on 2026-09-08 at the already established 01:00 Taipei
  time.
- Before that timestamp, the localized banner is a planned-release message,
  not a discount countdown.
- The no-JavaScript fallback also identifies 09 / 08 in all three languages.
- The existing Steam destination and post-launch offer behavior remain
  unchanged.
