Desktop Pet Studio 是一款在 Windows 裝置上執行的桌面寵物工具。本政策適用於程式、本官方網站，以及程式提供的選用 Google Calendar 同步功能。

1. 我們處理的資料

桌寵名稱、姿勢素材路徑、動畫設定、提醒、任務、照顧日常、快捷鍵、免打擾設定與本機工作坊內容，會保存在你的裝置上，讓程式能運作並保留你的設定。

本官方網站是靜態網站，未內建廣告追蹤、第三方分析工具或帳號註冊表單。

2. 支援 Google 行事曆同步

你可以自行選擇在提醒頁使用 Google 同步。啟動後，桌面程式會在你的預設瀏覽器開啟 Google 登入與同意畫面；不會在內嵌瀏覽器中要求你輸入 Google 密碼。

程式只請求 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly 唯讀權限，用來讀取你選定日曆在同步範圍內的事件，並在你的裝置建立本機提醒。同步範圍可選近 30、60 或 180 天；程式啟動時與每天 00:00 會再次同步。

3. 保存、安全與刪除

Google 授權權杖使用 Windows DPAPI 保護並儲存在你的 Windows 使用者帳戶本機。事件資料會作為本機提醒保存於程式設定中；Desktop Pet Studio 不營運接收你行事曆資料的中介伺服器。

若你選用 Twitch 直播主模式，Twitch 授權權杖同樣以 Windows DPAPI 保護並只存在本機。Bits 事件、觀眾暱稱與訊息直接由 Twitch 連到你的電腦，僅用於當次桌寵反應；Desktop Pet Studio 不接收、不儲存或轉送這些資料。

你可在程式中中斷 Google 連線或更換帳號。中斷連線會移除本機授權資料並停止後續同步；既有的本機提醒會保留，直到你在程式中自行刪除或清除本機設定。

4. 分享與用途限制

我們不出售 Google 使用者資料、不將其用於廣告、不交給資料經紀商，也不使用 Google Calendar 資料訓練 AI。程式僅直接向 Google API 讀取提供同步所需的資料，不會將日曆內容傳送至我們經營的伺服器或用於與桌寵提醒無關的用途。

5. 你的選擇

Google Calendar 同步完全為選用功能。你可不登入 Google，仍使用鬧鐘、手動行事曆匯入、計時器與其他本機提醒功能。你也可隨時在 Google 帳戶的安全性設定撤銷應用程式存取權。

6. 政策更新與聯絡

若資料處理方式有重大變更，我們會在此頁更新生效日。你可先透過公開問題回報入口聯絡支援；正式 Google OAuth 驗證前會補上支援信箱。

Desktop Pet Studio is a desktop-pet tool that runs on Windows devices. This policy applies to the app, this official website, and the app's optional Google Calendar sync feature.

1. Information the app handles

Your pet name, pose asset paths, animation settings, reminders, tasks, care progress, hotkeys, do-not-disturb rules, and local workshop content are stored on your device so the app can work and remember your choices.

This official site is static. It does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or account-registration forms.

2. Google Calendar sync support

You may choose Google sync from the Reminders page. The desktop app opens Google sign-in and consent in your default browser; it does not ask you to enter a Google password in an embedded browser.

The app requests only the read-only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly scope. It reads events from the calendar you select within your chosen range and creates local reminders on your device. The available ranges are 30, 60, or 180 days; the app syncs at launch and again daily at 00:00.

3. Storage, security, and deletion

Google authorization tokens are protected with Windows DPAPI and stored in your local Windows user profile. Event information used for reminders is stored in local app configuration. Desktop Pet Studio does not operate an intermediary server that receives your calendar data.

If you use Twitch Streamer Mode, Twitch authorization tokens are also protected with Windows DPAPI and stay local. Bits events, viewer names, and messages travel directly from Twitch to your computer and are used only for the current pet reaction. Desktop Pet Studio does not receive, store, or forward that data.

You can disconnect Google or change accounts in the app. Disconnecting removes the local authorization data and stops future sync. Existing local reminders remain until you delete them in the app or clear local app settings.

4. Sharing and limits on use

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, provide it to data brokers, or use Google Calendar data to train AI. The app retrieves only the data needed to provide sync directly from Google APIs. It does not send calendar contents to a server we operate or use them for a purpose unrelated to desktop-pet reminders.

5. Your choices

Google Calendar sync is optional. You can use alarms, manual calendar imports, timers, and other local reminder features without signing in to Google. You can also revoke app access at any time from your Google Account security settings.

6. Policy changes and contact

If our data handling changes materially, we will update the effective date on this page. You can use the public issue-reporting channel for support; a support email will be added before Google OAuth verification.

A public support contact has not been published yet.
